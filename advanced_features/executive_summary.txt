
================================================================================
                    CHURN ANALYSIS - EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
================================================================================

OVERVIEW
--------
Dataset: 500 streaming service users
Overall Churn Rate: 38.6% (193 churned, 307 retained)

================================================================================
                        TOP 5 MAJOR CHURN DRIVERS
================================================================================

1. TOTAL VIEWING HOURS (Importance: 23.8%)
   ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
   • Churned users: 66.6 hours/month
   • Retained users: 83.2 hours/month
   • Difference: -20.0% (highly significant, p < 0.001)
   
   KEY INSIGHT: Lower engagement is the strongest predictor of churn.
   Users watching less than 70 hours/month are at HIGH RISK.

2. AVERAGE SESSION DURATION (Importance: 21.6%)
   ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
   • Churned users: 49.4 minutes/session
   • Retained users: 57.8 minutes/session  
   • Difference: -14.4% (highly significant, p < 0.001)
   
   KEY INSIGHT: Shorter sessions indicate lower content satisfaction.
   Sessions under 50 minutes signal disengagement.

3. NUMBER OF UNIQUE TITLES WATCHED (Importance: 15.9%)
   ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
   • Churned users: 19.5 titles/month
   • Retained users: 23.7 titles/month
   • Difference: -18.1% (highly significant, p < 0.001)
   
   KEY INSIGHT: Content diversity matters significantly.
   Users exploring fewer titles are more likely to churn.

4. TOP GENRE (Importance: 11.4%)
   ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
   Churn rates by genre:
   • Horror: 52.3% (HIGHEST RISK)
   • Thriller: 48.3%
   • Action: 44.6%
   • Romance: 41.8%
   • SciFi: 40.5%
   • Drama: 35.3%
   • Comedy: 33.0%
   • Documentary: 25.9% (LOWEST RISK)
   
   KEY INSIGHT: Content preference impacts retention.
   Horror/Thriller fans show 2x churn vs Documentary viewers.

5. CUSTOMER SERVICE INTERACTIONS (Importance: 9.1%)
   ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
   • Churned users: 3.18 interactions/year
   • Retained users: 2.49 interactions/year
   • Difference: +27.7% (highly significant, p < 0.001)
   
   KEY INSIGHT: More CS contacts = Higher churn risk.
   Each additional interaction increases churn likelihood.
   Users with 4+ interactions have 50%+ churn rate.

================================================================================
                        ADDITIONAL FINDINGS
================================================================================

BINGE WATCHING SESSIONS (Importance: 11.2%)
   • Churned: 6.2 sessions/month vs Retained: 7.7 sessions/month
   • -19.7% difference (p < 0.001)
   • Inverse relationship with churn

SUBSCRIPTION TIER (Importance: 3.5%)
   • Basic tier: 43.5% churn rate (HIGHEST)
   • Standard tier: 39.5% churn rate
   • Premium tier: 24.1% churn rate (LOWEST)
   • Chi-square test significant (p = 0.009)

MONTHLY COST (Importance: 3.6%)
   • Churned: $11.18 vs Retained: $12.11
   • -7.7% difference (p = 0.005)
   • Lower cost correlates with higher churn (tier effect)

================================================================================
                        KEY ACTIONABLE INSIGHTS
================================================================================

🎯 PRIMARY RISK FACTORS (Immediate Action Required):
   1. Users viewing < 70 hours/month
   2. Average session duration < 50 minutes
   3. Watching < 20 unique titles/month
   4. 4+ customer service interactions/year
   5. Basic tier subscribers

📊 ENGAGEMENT SCORE ANALYSIS:
   • Created composite engagement metric (viewing + binge + diversity)
   • Low engagement (bottom 30%): ~60% churn rate
   • High engagement (top 30%): ~15% churn rate
   • 4x difference between extremes

💡 STRATEGIC RECOMMENDATIONS:

   1. PROACTIVE MONITORING:
      → Flag users with declining viewing hours
      → Identify short session patterns early
      → Track content diversity metrics

   2. TARGETED INTERVENTIONS:
      → Personalized content recommendations for low-diversity viewers
      → Re-engagement campaigns for users < 70 hours/month
      → Premium upgrade incentives for Basic tier at-risk users

   3. CUSTOMER SERVICE:
      → Investigate root causes of CS interactions
      → Resolve issues before multiple contacts occur
      → CS contact is a strong leading indicator

   4. CONTENT STRATEGY:
      → Strengthen Horror/Thriller catalog quality
      → Promote Documentary content (lowest churn)
      → Encourage content exploration

   5. PRICING/TIER OPTIMIZATION:
      → Review Basic tier value proposition
      → Consider Premium tier benefits promotion
      → Price sensitivity appears LOW (cost is minor factor)
